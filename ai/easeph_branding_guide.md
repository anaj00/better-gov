# EasePH Branding Guide
## For the Demo Website and Product UI

**Document type:** Branding and visual identity guide  
**Purpose:** Define the branding direction for the EasePH demo website and supporting UI screens  
**Status:** Draft for implementation in Codex  

---

## 1. Brand Overview

EasePH is a government process platform designed to make business-related government processes feel easier, clearer, and more accessible.

The brand should feel:

- Efficient
- Trustworthy
- Modern
- Simple
- Civic
- Confident
- Approachable

It should not feel:

- Bureaucratic
- Outdated
- Overly corporate
- Overdecorated
- Too playful
- Heavy or cluttered

The visual identity should communicate:

> Government processes, made easier.

---

## 2. Brand Positioning

### Core Idea

EasePH helps businesses request, track, and complete government processes more easily.

### Brand Promise

A simpler, clearer, and more transparent way to navigate government requirements.

### Brand Personality

EasePH should feel like:

- A modern civic platform
- A helpful guide
- A reliable government-tech interface
- A service that reduces friction

### Tone

Use a tone that is:

- Direct
- Clear
- Reassuring
- Minimal
- Helpful

Avoid language that is:

- Too technical
- Too stiff
- Too bureaucratic
- Too salesy

---

## 3. Logo

The provided EasePH logo should be treated as the main brand mark.

### Logo Direction

The logo uses:

- Deep blue as the primary color
- Red as the accent color
- A bold, high-energy visual style

This gives EasePH a more energetic and memorable identity compared with the earlier softer blue-green version.

### Logo Usage Guidance

Use the provided EasePH logo:

- In the website header
- On authentication pages
- On exported or downloadable materials
- On the receipt page, if needed
- On presentation screens

### Clear Space

Maintain comfortable spacing around the logo.

A safe rule:

- Keep at least the height of the `PH` letters as padding around the logo

### Minimum Usage Rule

Do not place the logo in crowded or low-contrast areas.

Prefer:

- White background
- Very light neutral backgrounds
- Simple headers with enough breathing room

### Do Not

- Stretch the logo
- Rotate the logo
- Recolor the logo arbitrarily
- Add effects such as glow, bevel, or heavy shadows
- Place it on noisy backgrounds

---

## 4. Tagline

Recommended tagline:

> **Government Processes, Made Easy**

This tagline should appear:

- Under or beside the logo in the header
- In branding areas when appropriate
- In the footer if needed

It should not be overused throughout the page.

---

## 5. Color System

The homepage direction should use a **blue and red** brand system.

### Primary Colors

#### Brand Blue
Use as the main brand color.

Suggested usage:

- Header CTAs
- Primary buttons
- Links
- Navigation emphasis
- Icons
- Charts
- Key UI accents

Suggested hex:
- `#0B2E8A` or a very similar rich royal/navy blue

#### Brand Red
Use as the main accent color.

Suggested usage:

- Highlight word in hero heading
- Secondary emphasis
- Accent dividers
- Select icons
- Certain data highlights
- Small UI emphasis points

Suggested hex:
- `#D61F3A` or a similar vivid red

### Supporting Colors

#### Dark Text
- `#0F172A` or similar dark navy/charcoal

Use for:

- Main headings
- High-emphasis text

#### Body Text
- `#475569` or similar muted slate

Use for:

- Supporting paragraph text
- Labels
- Explanatory copy

#### Light Background
- `#F8FAFC` or near-white
- `#FFFFFF`

Use for:

- Main page background
- Cards
- Sections

#### Border / Divider
- `#E2E8F0`

Use for:

- Subtle borders
- Section dividers
- Card outlines

#### Soft Blue Tint
- `#EEF4FF`

Use for:

- Soft background chips
- Highlight cards
- Light icon containers

#### Soft Red Tint
- `#FDECEE`

Use for:

- Accent icon containers
- Highlight states when needed

### Color Principles

- Blue should dominate.
- Red should be used as emphasis, not as the base color.
- The interface should still feel calm and readable.
- Avoid using too many additional colors beyond blue, red, white, and neutrals.

---

## 6. Typography

The product should use a clean modern sans-serif typeface.

Suggested fonts:

- **Inter**
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**
- **Manrope**

Preferred choice:

- **Inter** for implementation simplicity and clarity

### Typography Direction

#### Hero Heading
- Very large
- Bold
- Clean
- High contrast
- Minimal line count

Example style:
- 56–72px on desktop
- 700–800 weight
- Tight line height

#### Section Headings
- Strong but smaller than hero
- 28–40px
- Bold

#### Body Copy
- Clean and readable
- 16–20px
- Regular to medium weight

#### Labels and UI Text
- 14–16px
- Medium weight
- Clear and not overly condensed

### Typography Style Notes

- Keep headlines concise
- Keep paragraph copy short
- Use whitespace to create elegance
- Avoid long marketing paragraphs

## 7. Homepage Branding Direction

The homepage should establish the brand clearly and immediately.

### Core Homepage Layout

The approved direction is:

1. **Clean top navigation**
2. **Hero section first**
3. **Two main CTAs**
4. **Right-side illustration**
5. **Compact stats strip below**

### Header

The header should include:

- EasePH logo on the left
- Navigation items:
  - Home
  - Processes
  - National Stats
- A top-right primary CTA:
  - Request a Process

Do not include `Agency Login` in the main homepage header.

### Hero Messaging

Keep the hero copy minimal.

Recommended style:

#### Headline
> **Government processes, made easier.**

or similar variations that are equally short and strong.

#### Supporting copy
Keep it to 1–2 lines only.

Example:
> Request, track, and complete processes in just a few simple steps.

### Hero CTAs

Use exactly two main CTAs:

#### Primary CTA
- **Request a Process**

#### Secondary CTA
- **View National Stats**

The primary CTA should be a filled blue button.  
The secondary CTA should be an outlined or lighter button.

### Hero Visual

The right side should contain:

- A stylized government building
- Philippine flag
- Soft city skyline
- A process status card showing simple progress

The illustration should feel:

- Premium
- Clean
- Slightly aspirational
- Not cartoonish
- Not overly detailed

---

## 8. UI Style Principles

### Overall Look

The interface should be:

- Bright
- Minimal
- Spacious
- Rounded
- Modern
- Professional

### Spacing

Use generous spacing.

The website should feel:

- Uncluttered
- Premium
- Easy to scan

### Cards

Cards should have:

- White backgrounds
- Rounded corners
- Very light borders or shadows
- Clean internal spacing

### Buttons

#### Primary Button
- Solid blue fill
- White text
- Rounded corners
- Medium to large size
- Right-arrow icon optional

#### Secondary Button
- White or very light background
- Blue border or subtle outline
- Blue text

#### Hover Behavior
Suggested:

- Slight elevation
- Slight darkening for primary
- Slight tint change for secondary

### Navigation

Keep navigation simple.

- Minimal top links
- Active item underlined or highlighted
- No heavy dropdowns in the hero state

### Icons

Use simple line or duotone icons.

Prefer:

- Shield
- Document
- Check
- Clock
- Chart
- Building

Do not use overly playful iconography.

---

## 9. Stats / Dashboard Preview Branding

The homepage includes a compact stats strip.

This section should not feel like a full dashboard.

It should serve as a **teaser** of the public stats system.

### Recommended Metrics

Use only a few metrics:

- Total Requests
- Completed
- Avg. Processing Time
- On-Time Rate

### Styling Notes

- Keep the section on a white rounded card
- Use icon circles
- Use large numbers
- Use small change indicators underneath
- Keep labels concise

### Data Color Logic

- Blue for general information
- Red for emphasis or time-related indicators
- Green only if absolutely necessary for directional stats, but keep usage restrained if sticking closely to blue/red branding

If a stricter brand system is preferred, directional arrows may use blue and red only.

## 10. Illustration Direction

The illustration style should match the homepage aesthetic.

### Illustration Characteristics

- Soft lighting
- Subtle gradients
- Architectural forms
- Slightly polished UI-illustration look
- Government building with a dignified but approachable feel
- Soft skyline in the background
- Clean reflective or airy base

### Important Visual Elements

- Government building
- Philippine flag
- Trees or soft civic landscaping
- Floating request-status card

### Avoid

- Busy scenes
- Many small UI cards
- Overly literal dashboards
- Cartoon people
- Overly complex isometric scenes

## 13. Implementation Notes for Codex

When implementing the homepage and UI, Codex should follow these branding rules:

1. Use the uploaded EasePH logo.
2. Use a blue-and-red visual system.
3. Keep the homepage minimal and hero-led.
4. Use only two hero CTAs:
   - Request a Process
   - View National Stats
5. Put `Request a Process` in the top-right header as the main CTA.
6. Do not show `Agency Login` on the homepage.
7. Keep copy short and visually clean.
8. Use a civic/government building illustration on the right side.
9. Keep the bottom metrics strip compact.
10. Prioritize whitespace and a premium feel.

EasePH should feel like:

> **A modern Philippine government process platform that is cleaner, clearer, and easier to use.**

The brand direction is:

- **Blue and red**
- **Minimal**
- **Hero-first**
- **Trustworthy**
- **Modern civic**
- **Simple and high-clarity**

The homepage should communicate the brand immediately through:

- The logo
- The short headline
- The two CTAs
- The government illustration
- The compact national stats preview
